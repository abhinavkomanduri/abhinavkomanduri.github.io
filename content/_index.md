My research interests span **embedded systems**, **wearable sensing**, and **ubiquitous computing**, with a focus on low-cost IoT systems for clinically relevant health applications.

I am a **2026 NSF Graduate Research Fellowship (GRFP)** recipient and a **2025 Barry Goldwater Scholar**. I also received an **Honorable Mention** for the CRA Outstanding Undergraduate Researcher Award. In summer 2025, I was a visiting researcher at the **Georgia Institute of Technology** in the [Inan Research Lab](https://inanlab.gatech.edu/) under Dr. Omer Inan, developing deep-learning models for wearable physiological signal analysis.
